FAQ

■ Most Common Questions

No worries, here you can find all the answers

Who can join the Sensing and Automation Club (SAC)?
+
Any student with a passion for robotics, AI, or building hardware can join SAC! We welcome all skill levels, from beginners looking to learn, to advanced developers ready to lead a project.
Do I need prior experience in robotics or AI?
+
Not at all. SAC provides mentorship, workshops, and team-based learning. As long as you have the curiosity and dedication to learn, you will quickly pick up the skills you need.
What kind of projects does the club build?
+
We build everything from autonomous drones and IoT smart-home devices to computer vision systems and advanced robotics. Our portfolio is shaped by the ideas our members bring to the table.
How do I pitch my own project idea?
+
Once you become a member, you can submit a project proposal during our bi-weekly incubation sessions. If approved, the club provides funding, lab space, and team support to help you build it.
When and where do we meet?
+
We will let you know according to the need !

Sensing and Automation Club

SAC BTKIT is the Sensing and Automation Club at Bipin Tripathi Kumaon Institute of Technology, Dwarahat, Uttarakhand. Explore robotics, sensing, embedded systems and automation through student projects, workshops and club events.
